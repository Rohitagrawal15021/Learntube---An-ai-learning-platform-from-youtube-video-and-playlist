How it works

Three steps, in order. The middle one is where the time goes.

  1. 1

    Paste a link

    A single lecture, or a whole playlist. Any language — the transcript is translated to English first, so the notes read cleanly.

  2. 2

    It reads the whole thing

    Filler and [Music] tags are stripped, the transcript is split at sentence boundaries, and each chunk is rewritten into notes — then, for a playlist, linked into one knowledge map.

  3. 3

    Revise

    Notes, summary, flashcards, and a quiz in one workspace. Miss a question and it sends you to the exact second in the video to rewatch.
